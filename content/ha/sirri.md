## A taƙaice

> **skeletonic.com ba ta saita cookies na bin diddigi kuma ba ta gudanar da alamar bincike ta wasu ba.** Gidan yanar gizon tarin fayilolin HTML, CSS, da fayil guda na service-worker ne. Babu buƙatar da kuke yi da aka rubuta ban da bayanan shiga na GitHub Pages na yau da kullun.

<hr class="hr-text" data-content="Cikakkun bayanai">

## Waɗanne bayanai ake tattarawa

1. **Bayanan shiga na GitHub Pages.** Lokacin da kuka loda kowanne shafi, GitHub yana adana adireshin IP ɗin ku, URL na buƙata, da lokacin. Manufar sirri ta GitHub ta shafi — duba [docs.github.com/en/site-policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
2. **Sigar tuntuɓar.** Idan kuka aika sigar a `/ha/tuntubi/`, bayanan suna wucewa ta [Formspree](https://formspree.io), wanda ke tura su zuwa imel ɗin mai kula. Manufar sirri ta Formspree ta shafi.
3. **Service worker.** Rubutun `sw.js` yana adana amsoshin HTML, CSS, JS, da hotuna **a cikin burauzar ku** don gidan yanar gizon ya yi aiki ba tare da yanar gizo ba. Ba a aika komai ko'ina.

## Abin da *ba* a tattara ba

- Babu Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, ko wata alamar bincike.
- Babu cookies na kowane iri. Gidan yanar gizon ba ya saita koɗanne.
- Babu local storage ko IndexedDB ban da cache na service-worker.
- Babu bin sawu, hanyoyin talla, ko masu bin diddigi na wasu.
