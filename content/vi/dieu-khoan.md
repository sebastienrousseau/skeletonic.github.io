## 1. Thu vien

**Skeletonic Stylus** duoc cap phep kep theo
[Giay phep MIT](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT) va
[Giay phep Apache 2.0](https://opensource.org/licenses/Apache-2.0). Ban
co the su dung, sao chep, sua doi, hop nhat, xuat ban, phan phoi, cap
phep phu va/hoac ban cac ban sao cua phan mem, theo dieu khoan cua
mot trong hai giay phep.

Thu vien duoc cung cap **"NGUYEN TRANG", khong co bao hanh duoi bat ky
hinh thuc nao**, minh thi hoac ngam dinh, bao gom nhung khong gioi han
cac bao hanh ve tinh thuong mai, su phu hop voi muc dich cu the va
khong vi pham.

## 3. Nhan hieu

"Skeletonic" va logo Skeletonic la nhan hieu chua dang ky cua
Sebastien Rousseau. Ban co the su dung chung de chi du an, nhung
vui long khong su dung theo cach goi y su ung ho cho san pham
khong lien quan.
