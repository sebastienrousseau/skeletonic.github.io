## Tom tat

> **skeletonic.com khong dat cookie theo doi va khong chay the phan tich
> cua ben thu ba.** Trang web la tap hop tinh cac tap HTML, CSS va mot
> tap service-worker duy nhat. Khong yeu cau nao cua ban duoc ghi lai
> ngoai nhat ky truy cap GitHub Pages tieu chuan.

<hr class="hr-text" data-content="Chi tiet">

## Du lieu nao duoc thu thap

1. **Nhat ky truy cap GitHub Pages.** Khi ban tai bat ky trang nao,
   GitHub ghi lai dia chi IP, URL yeu cau va thoi gian. Chinh sach
   quyen rieng tu cua GitHub ap dung — xem
   [docs.github.com/en/site-policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
2. **Bieu mau lien he.** Neu ban gui bieu mau tren `/vi/lien-he/`, du
   lieu di qua [Formspree](https://formspree.io), chuyen tiep den email
   cua nguoi bao tri. Chinh sach quyen rieng tu cua Formspree ap dung.
3. **Service worker.** Script `sw.js` luu tru HTML, CSS, JS va phan hoi
   hinh anh **cuc bo trong trinh duyet cua ban** de trang web hoat dong
   offline. Khong co gi duoc gui di bat cu dau.

## Nhung gi *khong* duoc thu thap

- Khong Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, hoac bat ky the phan tich nao khac.
- Khong cookie duoi bat ky hinh thuc nao. Trang web khong dat cookie.
- Khong local storage hoac IndexedDB ngoai bo nho cache service-worker.
- Khong fingerprinting, mang quang cao, hoac trinh theo doi ben thu ba.

## Quyen cua ban

Neu ban da su dung bieu mau lien he va muon tin nhan bi xoa, gui email
tu dia chi ban da gui va toi se xoa trong vong 30 ngay.
