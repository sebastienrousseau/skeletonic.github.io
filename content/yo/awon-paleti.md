Skeletonic Stylus v2.0.0 fi **awọn palẹti awọ yan mẹta** ranṣẹ, ọkọọkan
ni a pin bi iwe-aṣa ti a dinku tirẹ nitorinaa o san fun ohun ti o lo nikan.

| Palẹti | Faili | Iwọn (min) |
|---|---|---|
| Material | `dist/css/palettes/material/skeletonic-material.min.css` | ≈17.4 KB |
| Tachyons | `dist/css/palettes/tachyons/skeletonic-tachyons.min.css` | 7.3 KB |
| Aabo wẹẹbu | `dist/css/palettes/websafe/skeletonic-websafe.min.css` | ≈3.5 KB |

Oju-iwe yii n gba palẹti **Tachyons** nitorinaa awọn kilasi iwulo awọ
ni isalẹ ṣe afihan ni deede.

## Awọn awọ ami iyasọtọ (v2.0.0 — iyatọ AA)

Ninu v2.0.0 awọn ami akọkọ ati keji ni a ṣe dudu lati pade
**WCAG 4.5:1** iyatọ si ọrọ funfun lẹsẹkẹsẹ.

> si funfun — o ni ibamu AA fun **ọrọ nla** (o kere ju 18 px nipọn /

> awọn ipilẹ dudu tabi lo fun awọn ẹya ọṣọ ti kii ṣe pataki nikan.

## Awọn kilasi iwulo palẹti Tachyons

Nigba ti o ba gba `skeletonic-tachyons.min.css` o gba eto pipe ti awọn
kilasi awọ idi-kan — ohun kanna ti a fi ranṣẹ, modulu yan.

```html
<span class="bg-blue white pa2">.bg-blue .white</span>
<span class="bg-light-green dark-green pa2">.bg-light-green .dark-green</span>
<span class="bg-washed-yellow dark-gray pa2">.bg-washed-yellow .dark-gray</span>
```

Pato palẹti Tachyons ni kikun ni a ṣe akọsilẹ ni
[tachyons.io/docs/themes/skins](https://tachyons.io/docs/themes/skins/);
ẹya ti a fi ranṣẹ nibi ni eyi ti Skeletonic ṣajọ.
