Alamomi

Amsa
 
Saman da ke gaya wa mai ziyara cewa wani abu ya faru — ko kuma yana shirin faruwa.
 
Faɗakarwa
 
Saƙonnin matsayi da niyyar ma'ana. A cikin v2.0.0 an sanya kowane nau'i a ƙarƙashin sunan yanki a ƙarƙashin <code>.alert-{primary,secondary,info,success,warning,error}</code> don haka ajin nau'i ba zai yi karo da azuzuwan yanayi a wani wuri a shafin ba.
 
<code class="language-html"><div class="alert alert-primary" role="alert"> <strong>Ku lura.</strong> Wannan faɗakarwa ce ta farko. </div> <div class="alert alert-success" role="status"> <strong>An ajiye.</strong> An adana canje-canjen ku. </div> <div class="alert alert-warning" role="alert"> <strong>A hankali.</strong> Wannan aikin yana shafar yanayin da ake raba. </div> <div class="alert alert-error" role="alert"> <strong>Kuskure.</strong> Ba a iya ajiye sigar ba. </div> </code></pre>

Bayanan samun dama. Yi amfani da <code>role="alert"</code> don saƙonnin da ke buƙatar kulawa nan take (kurakurai, gargaɗi) da <code>role="status"</code> don tabbatarwa marasa gaggawa. Duka biyun suna fallasa saƙon ga fasahar taimako a lokacin da ya bayyana.

Katunan

Tsari
 
Ginshiƙan tsari — grid, container, header — da ke ƙirƙira dukan shafin.
 
Grid
 
Grid ɗin <code>flex-N</code> mai amsa (1 → 12 ginshiƙi) da aka gina a kan flexbox. <code>.row</code> mai iyaye yana yin gap da nannaɗe ta atomatik.
 
<code class="language-html"><div class="row"> <div class="flex-6">Rabi</div> <div class="flex-6">Rabi</div> </div> <div class="row"> <div class="flex-4">Sulusi</div> <div class="flex-4">Sulusi</div> <div class="flex-4">Sulusi</div> </div> </code></pre>
