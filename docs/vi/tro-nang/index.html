Tro nang — WCAG 2.2 trong v2.0.0

Translation note (2026-04-30). v2.0.0 adds RTL support and a refreshed 2026 framework comparison. The English source is the canonical reference while these translations catch up. See https://skeletonic.io/about/ · https://skeletonic.io/components/ · https://skeletonic.io/benchmarks/.

Skeletonic Stylus v2.0.0 cung cap tuan thu WCAG 2.2 nhu hanh vi mac dinh, khong phai chu de tuy chon. Trang nay ghi lai nhung gi duoc tich hop san va nhung mixin ban co the chon su dung de kiem soat chi tiet hon.

Nhung gi ban nhan duoc mien phi

WCAG 2.2 SC v2.0.0 lam gi Cach xac minh
1.4.3 Do tuong phan (Toi thieu) — AA <code>--cl-primary</code> va <code>--cl-secondary</code> duoc lam dam den ≥ 4,5:1 voi trang DevTools → kiem tra do tuong phan
1.4.11 Do tuong phan phi van ban — AA Vien bieu mau, nut bam, huy hieu deu ≥ 3:1 Tuong tu
2.4.7 Focus Hien thi — AA Moi phan tu co the focus deu co vong <code>:focus-visible</code> (vang tren nen toi, xanh tren nen sang) <code>Tab</code> qua bat ky trang nao
2.4.11 Focus Khong bi che — AA (moi trong 2.2) Header dinh co chieu cao giam + scroll-margin cho focus <code>Tab</code> qua header dinh
2.5.8 Kich thuoc muc tieu — AA (moi trong 2.2) Nut bam / lien ket: ≥ 24×24 px CSS vung cham DevTools box model
1.4.12 Gian cach van ban — AA Tat ca phan tu ton trong cac ghi de line-height / letter-spacing cua nguoi dung Ghi de trong DevTools
2.3.3 Hieu ung dong tu tuong tac — AAA Tat ca lop hieu ung dong duoc boc trong <code>@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)</code> Giam chuyen dong cap he dieu hanh
1.4.10 Tai bo cuc — AA Bo cuc hoat dong o 320 px khong co cuon ngang DevTools viewport hep
Che do toi <code>prefers-color-scheme: dark</code> tu dong chuyen doi token Chuyen doi che do toi he dieu hanh

Stylus mixin ban co the chon su dung

Neu ban bien dich tu nguon, cac mixin sau co san trong <code>src/stylus/utilities/mixins.styl</code>:

Mixin Su dung
<code>focus-ring()</code> Vong focus do tuong phan cao ton trong <code>:focus-visible</code>
<code>visually-hidden()</code> Van ban chi danh cho trinh doc man hinh van co the focus
<code>skip-link()</code> Neo tro nen hien thi khi focus
<code>reduced-motion()</code> Boc bat ky khoi nao trong <code>prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference</code>
<code>forced-colors()</code> Dieu chinh cho Che do tuong phan cao Windows
<code>target-size(24px)</code> Dem bat ky phan tu nhan duoc den kich thuoc tap toi thieu

Tro giup lien ket bo qua

<code class="language-html"><a href="#main-content" class="skip-link">Chuyen den noi dung chinh</a> </code></pre>
