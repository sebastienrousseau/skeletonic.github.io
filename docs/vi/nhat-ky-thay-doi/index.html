Translation note (2026-04-30). v2.0.0 adds RTL support and a refreshed 2026 framework comparison. The English source is the canonical reference while these translations catch up. See https://skeletonic.io/about/ · https://skeletonic.io/components/ · https://skeletonic.io/benchmarks/.

v2.0.0 — 2026-04 (hien tai)

Dot ra soat "san sang phat hanh": tro nang, chuoi cung ung, ve sinh kho.

Tro nang (WCAG 2.2)

  • <code>$primary</code> duoc lam dam thanh <code>hsl(210, 100%, 42%)</code> cho do tuong phan AA voi chu trang trong nut bam / huy hieu / lien ket.
  • <code>$secondary</code> duoc lam dam thanh <code>hsl(195, 100%, 33%)</code>.
  • Them vong focus-visible tren moi phan tu tuong tac.
  • Them bo boc <code>@media (prefers-reduced-motion)</code> xung quanh mo-dun hieu ung dong.
  • Them chuyen doi token <code>prefers-color-scheme: dark</code>.
  • Them mixin target-size(24px) de dap ung WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8.
  • Them tro giup scroll-margin focus-not-obscured cho header dinh.

Build & chuoi cung ung

  • Cascade layers — moi khoi hien nam trong <code>@layer skeletonic.{reset,tokens,layout,elements,components,utilities}</code>.
  • CycloneDX SBOM duoc tao nhu mot phan cua tarball npm.
  • Ngan sach <code>size-limit</code> duoc thuc thi trong CI: gioi han 8 KB gzip cho stylesheet chinh.
  • CVE-2023-44270 (phan tich dong tra ve postcss) duoc va qua <code>pnpm.overrides</code>.
  • Provenance + ky tren quy trinh xuat ban npm (<code>--provenance --access public</code>).
  • Xoa plugin <code>stylelint-a11y</code> da luu tru khoi cau hinh lint.

Ve sinh kho & goi nguoi dung

  • Thu muc <code>package/</code> cu da bi xoa; <code>dist/</code> hien la nguon duy nhat cho nguoi dung.
  • README duoc viet lai phan anh duong cai dat v2.0.0.
  • <code>dist/</code> duoc commit; <code>debug/</code> duoc tao va gitignore.
  • <code>.github/workflows/npm-publish.yml</code> moi chay theo tag.

Sua loi (P0)

  • Hoi quy bo chon cha <code>row $:after</code> — thay the ky tu <code>$</code> lac boi <code>&</code> trong <code>src/stylus/components/_grid.styl</code>.
  • <code>.alternate</code> nham ap dung <code>animation-direction: reverse</code> thay vi <code>alternate</code>.
  • Dat ten <code>.alert-*</code> — <code>.alert.success</code> → <code>.alert.alert-success</code> de tranh xung dot voi cac lop trang thai.
