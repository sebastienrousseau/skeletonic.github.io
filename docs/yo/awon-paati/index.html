 Aiyipada Akọkọ Aṣeyọri Ikilọ Ewu 
 
 
 
Akiyesi irayẹ-si. Awọn aami jẹ ohun ọṣọ nipa aiyipada. Nigba ti aami ba gbe ami nikan (fun apẹẹrẹ iye ti a ko ka), fi i sinu oluranlọwọ ti a fi pamọ ni oju: <code>awọn ifiranṣẹ 3 ti a ko ka</code>.
 
 
 
Esi
 
Awọn oju ti o sọ fun alejo pe nkan kan ṣẹlẹ — tabi pe yoo ṣẹlẹ.
 
Awọn itaniji
 
Awọn ifiranṣẹ ipo pẹlu idi ti itumọ. Ninu v2.0.0 gbogbo ẹya ni a ṣe orukọ ni kedere labẹ <code>.alert-{primary,secondary,info,success,warning,error}</code> nitorinaa kilasi ẹya ko le kọlu pẹlu awọn kilasi ipo ni ibi miiran lori oju-iwe naa.
 
<code class="language-html"><div class="alert alert-primary" role="alert"> <strong>Heads up.</strong> This is a primary alert. </div> <div class="alert alert-success" role="status"> <strong>Saved.</strong> Your changes are persisted. </div> <div class="alert alert-warning" role="alert"> <strong>Careful.</strong> This action affects shared state. </div> <div class="alert alert-error" role="alert"> <strong>Error.</strong> Could not save the form. </div> </code></pre> 
 
 Akiyesi. Eyi jẹ itaniji akọkọ.

Awọn kaadi

Grid
